Across the leagues

We manage the off-field lives, brands, and businesses of elite athletes. True Path Management is dedicated to building, protecting, and scaling legacies both during and long after our clients’ competitive careers.

In the collegiate space, we provide comprehensive representation and contract negotiations for NIL athletes, helping young players maximize their market value and secure premium brand deals. For our professional athletes in the NFL, we pivot strictly to comprehensive off-field business management.

We do not negotiate NFL team contracts. Instead, we partner seamlessly with our clients’ certified contract agents to oversee their external brands, philanthropic foundations, youth camps, and daily lifestyle logistics outside the lines.

True Path Management works with athletes at every level of the game.

High School Athletes Recruiting & early brand foundation
College Athletes NIL representation & brand development
Professional Athletes Off-field business, brand & legacy management

Founder

Bio

Jamond DuBose
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Jamond DuBose founded True Path Management to give athletes the kind of guidance he wished he’d had. A former Division I football player who competed at three programs, earned starting roles at two, and was voted team captain at one, Jamond understands first-hand the pressures athletes face both on the field and off it, from managing their brand to protecting their long-term interests.

That experience shapes how True Path operates today. Jamond works as a professional athlete manager, helping clients navigate everything from brand and outsourced deal negotiation to lifestyle and career planning. His approach is built on a simple belief: the business he’s truly in is his clients’ peace of mind.
