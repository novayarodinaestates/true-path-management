The Founders

About Us

Møcean was created from trial and tribulation, navigating to an end goal. Persevering through, relentlessly.

SHYHEIM BATTLE

CO-CEO · MOCEAN

Four-star recruit out of Rocky Mount, NC who became one of the most experienced defensive backs in NC State Wolfpack history. Known for lockdown coverage, 5 career interceptions, and 41 career starts. Earned Honorable Mention All-ACC honors and represented the USA in international play.

Rocky Mount, North Carolina

Shyheim Battle's Story

From the red clay of Rocky Mount, North Carolina to the bright lights of Carter-Finley Stadium, Shyheim Battle built his reputation through relentless work and unshakeable discipline. A four-star recruit who became one of NC State's most decorated defensive backs, he logged 41 career starts and earned Honorable Mention All-ACC honors — proving that consistency and preparation separate the good from the elite.

When the final whistle blew on his playing career, Shyheim channeled that same intensity into building MOGAINZ — the fitness arm of MOCEAN. Drawing on years of D1 strength and conditioning, NFL Combine prep, and the mental toughness forged on the field, he created a training platform that meets people wherever they are and pushes them further than they thought possible. For Shyheim, the grind never stopped — it just found a new field.

ISAAC DUFFY

CO-CEO · MOCEAN

Walk-on turned scholarship athlete at NC State before transferring to UAlbany. In the 2021 Gator Bowl, playing out of position at safety with four starters down, Duffy delivered 13 tackles, a TFL, forced fumble, a PBU, and a sack. The definition of next man up.

Binghamton, New York

Isaac Duffy's Story

Isaac Duffy's journey is the definition of earning everything the hard way. Moving from upstate New York to North Carolina in pursuit of a football dream, he spent 2.5 years playing football in North Carolina as a 0-star athlete coming late to the recruiting scene. A walk-on at NC State who fought his way to a scholarship, he later transferred to UAlbany and proved that opportunity doesn't wait for perfect circumstances — it rewards those who refuse to quit. He then turned his career pro after completing a season in the IFL. Representing 607, his shine-in-the-dark & hard work mentality brought him to his dream — becoming the blueprint for everything MOCEAN stands for.

After football, Isaac took that same fearless mentality off the field and into the world of business. As Co-CEO of MOCEAN, Duffy doesn't just talk about building channels for others; he walks through them first, proving the path is real.
