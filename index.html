Off-Field Management

We manage the off-field lives, brands, and businesses of elite athletes. For our professional clients, that means comprehensive business management outside the lines. That covers external brands, philanthropic foundations, youth camps, appearances, and daily lifestyle logistics, so the athlete can focus on the game.

How we fit. True Path manages everything outside the professional playing contract, and works collaboratively with each athlete’s certified contract advisor.

Financial Guidance & Referrals

Athletes should learn the money side early. We connect our clients — collegiate athletes in particular — with vetted, independent financial advisors who can help them understand, protect, and grow what they earn.

To be clear. True Path Management does not manage, invest, structure, or advise on any client’s personal finances. We make introductions and help athletes build the right professional team around them. All financial planning, advice, and decisions rest with the athlete and their licensed advisor.

Introductions to licensed advisors — never financial advice from us
