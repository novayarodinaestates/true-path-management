Privacy Policy

How True Path Management collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

Last updated September 2026  ·  Applies to truepathmgmt.org

1. Who we are

True Path Management (“True Path,” “we,” “us”) is an athlete management company. This policy covers information collected through this website. You can reach us at info@truepathmgmt.org or 919-417-7560.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. There is no account system and no login on this site.

Information you submit

When you use our contact form, we collect the fields you complete. Depending on whether you identify as an athlete, a family member, or a brand, that may include:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • The type of enquiry you are making
  • For athletes and families: sport, level, current team or school, Instagram handle, and the type of support you are seeking
  • For brands: company name, campaign type, budget range, timeline, and the athlete or sport you are interested in
  • Anything you write in the message field

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard technical information when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp. This is used for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this information to build a profile of you.

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry
  • To assess whether our services are a fit for your situation
  • To contact you about a potential working relationship
  • To keep records of business enquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to send marketing unrelated to your enquiry.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that make this site work, and only to the extent they need it:

  • Web3Forms processes and delivers our contact form submissions by email.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site, which involves a request to Google’s servers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property. If True Path is involved in a merger or acquisition, information may transfer as part of that transaction.

5. Athletes under 18

We work with high school athletes, so this section matters. This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If the athlete is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should submit the contact form on their behalf. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us without appropriate consent, contact info@truepathmgmt.org and we will delete it promptly.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a reasonable business record, and longer where a law, contract, or dispute requires it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

Email info@truepathmgmt.org and we will respond within a reasonable period. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to object to certain processing and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

8. Security

Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information — financial account details, government identification numbers, or medical information — through the contact form.

9. Links to other sites

This site links to third-party sites, including Instagram. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and this policy does not apply to them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted more prominently.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request about your information:
info@truepathmgmt.org  ·  919-417-7560
